# Blind King

Solve chess puzzles blindfold, using only your ears and your voice.

Blind King reads a position or a sequence of moves aloud, listens to the move you say, and tells you whether it is correct. It is meant for practising visualisation away from a board, including in the car.

## Goals

- **Read the board aloud**, so no screen or board is needed to follow along.
- **Understand spoken moves** and say whether each one is correct.
- **Offer four puzzle modes**:
  - Single position: find the correct move.
  - Continuation: play several moves in a row. If a puzzle has different variations, each one is answered independently.
  - From the start: the app reads the moves from the starting position, then pauses and asks what White or Black should play next.
  - Hybrid: starting from a position in the middle of a game, the app reads a few moves, then stops at the point where you must find the move.
- **Let you replay the narration**, with controls to go back one move, go forward one move, rewind to the start and jump to the end.
- **Provide different difficulty levels and themes.**
- **Use puzzles from [Lichess](https://lichess.org).**

There is nothing to watch: no board display and no animations.

## Status

Early setup. Blind King is being built as a web app. A native iOS app with CarPlay support is the longer-term ambition and may follow later.
